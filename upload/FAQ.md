# Cooperative Bank of Oromia (CBO) – Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

## General Information

### 1. What is Cooperative Bank of Oromia?

Cooperative Bank of Oromia (CBO) is a financial institution in Ethiopia that provides banking services including savings, loans, digital banking, and investment solutions.

### 2. When was CBO established?

CBO was established in 2004 to promote financial inclusion and cooperative banking services in Ethiopia.

### 3. Who owns CBO?

The bank is primarily owned by cooperative societies, unions, and individual shareholders.

---

## Accounts

### 4. What types of accounts does CBO offer?

CBO offers:

* Savings Accounts
* Current Accounts
* Fixed Deposit Accounts
* Youth and Women-focused Accounts

### 5. What documents are required to open an account?

To open an account, you typically need:

* Valid ID (Kebele ID, Passport, or Driver’s License)
* Passport-size photographs
* Initial deposit

### 6. Can I open an account online?

Yes, CBO supports digital account opening through its mobile banking platform (subject to eligibility).

---

## Digital Banking

### 7. What digital banking services does CBO provide?

CBO offers:

* Mobile Banking App
* Internet Banking
* USSD Banking
* ATM Services

### 8. How do I register for mobile banking?

You can register by visiting a branch or using the CBO mobile app and following the registration steps.

### 9. Is mobile banking secure?

Yes, CBO uses encryption, PIN protection, and authentication mechanisms to ensure secure transactions.

---

## Loans and Financing

### 10. What types of loans are available?

CBO provides:

* Personal Loans
* Business Loans
* Agricultural Loans
* Microfinance Loans

### 11. What are the requirements for getting a loan?

Requirements may include:

* Valid identification
* Proof of income or business
* Collateral (depending on loan type)

### 12. How long does loan approval take?

Loan approval time varies but typically ranges from a few days to a couple of weeks.

### 13. Does CBO provide ATM or debit cards?

Yes, customers receive ATM/debit cards for account access and transactions.

### 14. Can I use my CBO card internationally?

International usage depends on the card type and bank policies.
